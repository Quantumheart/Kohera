// Open network

Built on Matrix

Matrix is an open standard for secure, decentralized communication. That means you are not locked into Kohera — or any single company.

Choose your server

Sign in with any Matrix homeserver, or run your own. Your account and contacts stay on the server you trust.

Talk to anyone

Message people using other Matrix apps. No walled gardens. No forced app downloads for your contacts.

Leave anytime

Your data is portable. Switch to another Matrix client without losing your account, rooms, or message history.

// Privacy

Your conversations belong to you

Encrypted by default

Kohera does not run servers that store your messages or contacts. Your account lives on the Matrix homeserver you choose. Messages and calls are protected with end-to-end encryption. There are no ads, analytics, tracking, or telemetry SDKs.

// FAQ

Common questions

Do I need a phone number?

No. You can sign up with just a username and password on a Matrix homeserver of your choice.

Is Kohera really free?

Yes. Kohera is open-source software released under the GPL-3.0 license. There are no subscriptions, ads, or premium tiers.

Can I use my existing Matrix account?

Yes. Just enter your existing homeserver and credentials. Your rooms, contacts, and history will be there.

Who can see my messages?

In encrypted rooms, only the participants can read them — not Kohera, not the homeserver operator, and not any third party.
